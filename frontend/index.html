How It Works

A transparent 3-step verification architecture connecting AI intelligence with blockchain immutability.
1 · Check

AI Fraud Engine

The backend analyzes price per sq ft against locality benchmarks, screens descriptions for advance-fee scam terms, and calculates perceptual hashing (pHash) on photos.

2 · Verify

Oracle Signature

Listings scoring 70+ receive a tamper-proof cryptographic ECDSA signature from the AI oracle. Because only the oracle can sign verified hashes, listers cannot forge scores.

3 · Record On-Chain

Sepolia Proof

The listing hash (including location coordinates) and image hashes are permanently minted to PropertyVerifier.sol on Ethereum Sepolia. Anyone can publicly verify the listing on Etherscan or file community reports.
